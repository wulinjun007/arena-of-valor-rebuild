## Hero data verification

Hero IDs were mapped to names by reading the official 504×430 hero cards
(each card carries the hero name + title burned into the artwork). Roles follow the
official six-class filter taxonomy (Tank / Warrior / Assassin / Mage / Marksman /
Support); cross-checked against the Arena of Valor community wiki
(arenaofvalor.fandom.com).

If you are a rights holder and want any asset removed, please open an issue on the
repository and it will be taken down promptly.
