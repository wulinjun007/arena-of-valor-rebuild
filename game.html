WHAT IS
ARENA OF VALOR?

Welcome! Glad that you are interested in Arena of Valor. Arena of Valor is a multiplayer online battle arena game designed for mobile devices. Players are formed into two even teams — RED & BLUE — and whoever destroys the base of the opposing side first gets victory. To reach the enemy base, you manage the hero you selected, fight against opponents, and take down the Defense Towers along at least one of the three paths.

Classic 5V5 is the most popular mode in Arena of Valor — show your excellent skill and explore strategies with your team. Before battle starts, you select a hero to fight for you: muscular Tanks who rush in first and protect teammates like a shield, mysterious Assassins with high agility who take enemies' heads in a blink, or Marksman and Mage classes for the pure joy of massive damage from distance. And don't worry — there are Supports like Peura, whose healing power can assist and protect you.

As a mobile MOBA, Arena of Valor is optimized for touch: control wheels with your left hand to move your hero, and tap buttons on the right side to cast abilities.
